## The brief

Ekowe needed a proper web presence quickly: somewhere new listeners could understand the band, hear the latest release, find upcoming shows and get in touch. The site had to feel like the band, rather than a generic music template, and be ready for the client's immediate need.

## The approach

The website was designed and built in under a day using AstroJS and Tailwind CSS. A dark, high-contrast visual system, expressive display typography and the band's own photography give the site a clear identity from the first screen.

The delivery includes:

- A performance-focused static AstroJS site deployed on Cloudflare Workers.
- Responsive pages for the homepage, music, shows, about and contact journeys.
- Direct links to Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube, Deezer, Amazon Music and Tidal.
- A structured events collection so new shows can be added as simple Markdown content.
- Reusable Astro components for navigation, event rows, streaming links, headings and the marquee release banner.
- Responsive image handling and optimised artwork for live photography, EP covers and single artwork.
- Accessible navigation, labels and external-link announcements across the site.
- Canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata and MusicGroup structured data for sharing and discovery.

## The outcome

Ekowe now has a live, maintainable home for its music, shows and contact details, delivered on the same day the need was identified. The site gives the band a memorable visual presence while keeping the important actions—listen, see shows and make contact—close at hand.
