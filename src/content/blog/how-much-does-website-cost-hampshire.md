Website pricing should follow the work involved, not a one-size-fits-all menu. A focused, content-ready static site can be an excellent fit for a small business that needs a clear online presence quickly. More involved sites need more discovery, content planning, design, and functionality.

## A focused five-page static site

When your offer, copy, images, and page structure are ready, a tightly scoped five-page static site can start around **£250–£500**. This is a fast, fixed-scope route for a simple home, about, services, contact, and supporting page.

## Most professional small-business websites

Most professional small-business projects start around **£2,500**. That budget allows for a more tailored process: clearer content structure, bespoke design decisions, stronger lead journeys, accessibility and performance work, and room to grow after launch.

## What affects the final quote?

- Number of pages and whether the content is ready to use.
- Whether you need a CMS, ecommerce, bookings, memberships, or integrations.
- Copywriting, photography, product data, and migration work.
- The level of design exploration and post-launch support required.
